# Frozen bounded SDF seam helper

Exactly two new files; no edits to WallGeometry, DifferentialRows, TimeStep, the collision provider or the asset. Apply `sdf-branches.patch` from the repository root. The module has no imports. The test uses the existing provider and Aorta collision asset. Twelve new tests and twenty-four tests combined with frozen WallGeometry passed.

## API and signs

`createCompositeWallSdfBranchesWorkspace(pointCount=2)` owns two reusable row buffers. Use pointCount=1 for a point, 2 for an already selected capsule sample.

`evaluateCompositeWallSdfBranches({field,face:{axis,gridIndex},positions,radius,contact,dofs?},out)` reads two adjacent cells from the exact quantized sparse storage and performs zero queries. `contact` must be the original `sparse-sdf` result. Capsule `segmentT` and integer `capsuleSampleCount` determine a fixed affine sample/support. Point queries use weight 1. Optional `dofs` supplies distinct global Int32 indices; omission gives local indices. Original contact values and normal are copied to `out.rawContact`; synthesized row normals have source `sparse-sdf-cell-polynomial`.

Each row has `gap`, `gapJacobian`=G, `normalForceColumn`=B, `forceColumn`=-B, `normalDerivative`=DB, `pointGapGradient`, `pointGapHessian`, `pointNormalDerivative`, `normal`, `gradientNorm`, `dofs`, `cell`, `lower`, `upper`, and `inOriginalCell`. `gapJacobian` and `normalForceColumn` are different arrays and different physical quantities. The mixed block contribution is Rq-=Fn*B, Kqq-=Fn*DB, KqFn=-B, and the active NCP geometry row is penalty*G. There is no force rescaling by |G|. Arrays are reused and belong to the workspace.

`out.supported` means exactly one proved **min/intersection** chart. `out.face`, `domain`, `jumpCorners`, `jumpLowerBound`, `jumpUpperBound`, `continuity`, `onSeam`, `selectedRow`, `sampleFraction`, and `weights` expose its proof and support. The chart consists of the interiors of the two neighboring cells plus their common face; other coordinate grid boundaries and the chart's two outer normal boundaries are excluded. The two polynomials may be evaluated as auxiliary continuations over this chart. A continuation does not acquire permission to carry a final force outside its original cell.

The sign proof deliberately requires both sets of occupancy bits uniformly inside. Raw signed distance and gap must match the selected polynomial, and the raw normal must match the selected normalized gradient. Missing/mixed/exterior sign proof is unsupported. Face continuity is checked directly. Strict positivity of all four bilinear derivative-jump corner values proves the min identity over the whole shared face. Smooth seams, max/union, unresolved order, zero/fallback gradients, coincident or opposite dependent normals, unavailable storage, source mismatches and multi-axis intersections receive distinct reasons. Unsupported rows are disabled and their differential arrays become NaN.

`measureCompositeWallSdfBranches(out,{forces:[FnL,FnR],penalty,gapTolerance,forceTolerance,workTolerance,about=[0,0,0]})` requires supported rows. It returns original/branch gaps, exact nonnegativity, projected NCP residual, complementarity, original-gap agreement, loaded-branch domain/tie admissibility, two forces, resultant, its magnitude, scalar force sum, nodal forces, local physical gradient, and moment about `about`. No tolerance is inferred. Any nonzero force on the wrong-domain continuation fails, even if tiny. Both normals may carry force only at the exact computational grid tie; there is no seam snap band. It is a **contact-only** certificate: the caller must requery the original provider and freshly evaluate this chart, then include these forces in its original elastic/inertial/length mechanical certificate. `converged` never means that a timestep passed.

`findCompositeWallSdfSeamCrossing({field,position,delta})` returns the earliest grid-plane event for a known affine sample path. It returns alpha, unmodified affine event point, axis/index/coordinate, direction, lower and upper cell indices. A starting-face event is explicitly alpha=0; the caller must consume it before further marching. A simultaneous or machine-unresolvable multi-axis cluster is unsupported. An event at a storage boundary without both adjacent cells is also unsupported. Invalid input/grid bounds throw. No point perturbation, provider call, collision winner search, committed time substep or timestep acceptance occurs.

## Intended caller responsibility

Refresh original source/sign/sample provenance on every trial. Keep two physical forces in the same local band block only for this proved chart. Ordinary NCP activity removes an open face; do not turn the grid face into a geometric wall x=constant at negative gap. A winner/sample/source change or a chart-boundary event requires a new original query/branch proof. Final acceptance uses the unchanged original gap and the explicit contact cone resultant together with original mechanical equations. Unsupported cases remain explicit failures or route to a separately certified model.

The preceding numerical diagnosis and reproducible Chain/inertia witness are frozen separately at `/tmp/oet-composite-sdf-seam-diagnosis-final-8996`. This module handles the kink of the discrete trilinear level set; it does not assert that a voxel face is an anatomical wall or an exact-distance ridge.
