# Independent audit of production nodal wall pressure

One actionable convergence bug was reproduced in the production prepared
JointTimeStep. A separate typed-ID issue found earlier in the audit was fixed
by root and its fix is independently verified in this snapshot. The new nodal
pressure law is reviewed as an intentional physical discretization, not as an
exact reduction of capsule pressure.

No existing production, test or prototype source was edited by this audit.
The immutable dependency snapshot is `/tmp/oet-production-nodal-wall-audit`.
`probe.mjs` has five independent assertion groups; its normal SDF fixture is
constructed independently. For the complete two-tool case it reuses the
existing actual JointFrictionTimeStep geometry/material helpers through a
snapshot-only helper that disables test registration and exports functions.
No production function is rewritten or instrumented.

## Open finding: prescribed separation cannot unload a loaded normal row

In the first production flat two-tool dt, both lumen and vessel Coulomb solve
successfully. Accepted vessel Fn is

`[0.38615348816463735, 0.16255508872752597, 0]`.

For the next dt, explicitly prescribe every catheter node to its own previous
position minus **1e−4 mm in y**, away from the upper stationary plane, and
hold both own catheter spins. The translation preserves both original rest
lengths exactly. Wire feed remains −.3, catheter feed remains 0, and the own
prepared material velocities/maps are generated by the existing test helper.
All six freshly queried original endpoint/capsule wall gaps are
**+0.00009999999999998899 mm**. There is no wall penetration or unknown
material history.

The production result rejects after one direction with status
`original-linear-equations`. It correctly rolls back; the bug is a false
convergence barrier, not acceptance of an invalid physical state.

The mechanism is specific:

- `JointWallRows` snapshot line293 chooses active `Fn−k*g>0` using the old
  positive pressure, leaving the equation `R=g`, `dR/dFn=0`.
- The actual pressure node now has exact physical position Dirichlet, so its
  geometry cannot move. `RelativeDirection` lines241–246 holds the redundant
  zero-geometry/zero-dual row at zero multiplier increment.
- The original row residual remains the positive fixed gap. The original
  linear-equation guard appropriately refuses to certify it, but no direction
  can switch the normal multiplier to the known physical solution Fn=0.

For this explicitly fixed, freshly proven open physical site, unilateral
contact uniquely requires **Fn=0 and Ft=0**. Prepare that known-inactive
condition or perform its exact algebraic back-substitution before requesting
a direction. This must be derived from the actual prescribed physical point
and fresh gap, not merely a zero free geometry Jacobian: on a curved wall a
current tangential derivative may vanish while second-order gap changes remain.
Do not weaken the residual gate, shift a pivot, or silently keep a positive
pressure at an open point.

The closed control, with identical new prescribed catheter positions, passes.
It retains the accepted nonzero pressure history, suppresses only the two
redundant prescribed catheter length rows `[1,3]`, and leaves the third site's
Fn/FtU/FtV exactly zero. Thus the correction must preserve the existing closed
Dirichlet pressure gauge; it should not zero all fixed-node reactions.

The exact failed proof and rollback assertion are preserved in `evidence.json`.
Relevant frozen source SHA256:

- JointWallRows: `a492b9f1aa8c334de26ba38989b464e9b8ad3c663f288ec33059a632110b88d3`
- JointTimeStep: `9a917835424c6aa66ff5e9a1e993dac2bbce26fd0be2edf577031b783da19749`
- WallFrictionRows: `cdcbfa956202f8779b47bda0267c20eb50d2c9682afc893032a197cc99cfa59b`

## Typed material IDs: fixed during audit

The initial live normal adapter used JSON.stringify on the complete owner
record and threw `TypeError: Do not know how to serialize a BigInt` for
`materialSegmentId:17n`, before the friction manager's typed encoder ran.
Root corrected the normal signature before the snapshot was captured.
The frozen probe verifies three distinct signatures for **17n**, **17** and
**"17"**, without coercion or aliasing. This issue is resolved in the frozen
JointWallRows source listed above; the old exception is a historical finding,
not a claimed failure of the frozen version.

## Preservation checks

The following independent probes pass:

1. **True sparse-SDF interior penetration.** On a quantized trilinear field,
   both physical pressure endpoints are strictly open with Fn=0 and local
   NCP=0. The original interior capsule gap is −.16875 mm. The complete normal
   certificate remains false and coverageMerit is positive. No capsule gap
   is silently represented by a pressure endpoint.
2. **Freshness across reuse and retry.** A genuinely open state can reuse the
   same original gaps with query:false and zero extra field queries. Its
   geometry values must match exactly. A new candidate, restore, or partial
   query-budget failure cannot reuse those gaps, commit, or provide surface
   geometry until a new complete original query succeeds. Partial failure
   leaves the caller's incoming residual unchanged. Fresh retry reproduces
   the original accepted normal history exactly.
3. **No friction queries and correct force indices.** One preparation and
   friction refresh consume zero extra provider calls or query callbacks.
   Normal indices are `[0,1,2]`. With explicit global normal offset7 and
   friction offset10, the six Ft cross-dual supports are exactly
   `[[7,11],[7,10],[8,13],[8,12],[9,15],[9,14]]`. A geometry mutation without
   a new original normal query revokes every friction row/load while
   preserving the input Fn/Ft history. Original refresh then restores
   identical maps and physical loads.
4. **Malformed input guards.** Numeric-string nodes, duplicate/missing sites,
   wrong incident traces and zero/NaN owner radii reject. No node/index
   coercion or radius repair is performed.
5. **Held-boundary and zero-load control.** The full production closed fixed
   catheter step passes with both original tool histories and expected
   pressure/traction dimensions. It retains loaded pressures and produces
   literal zero Fn and both Ft components at its zero-load third site.

Read-only source inspection also confirmed: nodal mode never enters the
normal envelope-transfer loop or duplicate-pressure loop; every owned
collector's original endpoint and capsule rows enter coverage on every
successful refresh; coverageMerit also enters the actual JointTimeStep line
search. Existing capsule/envelope pressure laws retain their own contracts.
Normal and friction histories include the explicit nodal law and chosen
one-sided ownership, and loaded law/trace reinterpretation rejects.

## Scope and reproduction

Run the repository wrapper:

```sh
node reports/composite-production-nodal-wall-audit/probe.mjs
```

It executes the frozen probe and writes fresh evidence to
`/tmp/oet-production-nodal-wall-audit-retest.json`. Source/dependency hashes,
the frozen executable, original evidence and helper derivation are recorded
in `manifest.json` and `source.json`. An expected reproduction of the open
Dirichlet finding is an assertion success in this audit; the probe's zero
exit status is **not** a claim that all production cases pass.

This audit does not rerun or certify the complete composite suite, benchmark
FPS, general catheter feed, hinge orientation transport, curved-wall contact
adaptation, or UI. The actual nodal combined fixture intentionally has
catheter feed0 and wire feed−.3. Production fixes after the listed snapshot
require their own retest; this evidence remains an immutable baseline.
